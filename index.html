How SensorMax Works

SensorMax separates mobile data acquisition from desktop visualization and analysis.

Step 1 Acquire

Android sensors collect measurement data with machine and measurement-point context.

Step 2 Visualize

Sensor data can be viewed through the desktop/web monitoring interface.

Step 3 Analyze

Recorded data can be processed for vibration, frequency, quality, and engineering research.

What to Open First

Start with the Live Dashboard to see the engineering interface. Open the Android App Demo to understand the acquisition side of the system.
